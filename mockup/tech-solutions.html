Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate with the tools we already use?

Yes. We start from your current stack and connect to it, whether it is a CRM, a database or internal systems.

Who owns the code and the content?

You do. The solution, the code and the content are delivered to you at the end of the project.

How long does a typical project take?

A website or an assistant is usually a matter of weeks; a custom app depends on scope. We define it in the proposal.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We can stay on as your technology partner with support, monitoring and improvements.

Can the assistant work in English and Spanish?

Yes. We design assistants and websites to work in both languages.
