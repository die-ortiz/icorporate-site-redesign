How we work

One team accountable for the whole event, with AI where it helps.

From the first conversation to the final report, the same people run every phase, so nothing gets lost between teams.

What the team delivers

Strategy & design

  • Goals, audience and format defined with you

    A short workshop to set objectives, target audience and the right format, so every later decision has a clear reference.

  • Budget and approvals

    A line-by-line budget and an approval flow, so you always know what is committed and what is pending.

  • Venue and vendor selection

    We shortlist, compare and negotiate venues and suppliers against your criteria and budget.

  • Floor plans and room diagrams

    Room layouts, capacities and attendee flows drawn up in advance, with options to compare before you decide.

  • Repeatable event templates

    Programs, checklists and timelines saved as a base, so the next edition starts from what worked.

  • Event identity and key messages

    Naming, visual line and core messages kept consistent across stage, web, app and printed material.

What the team delivers

Production & logistics

  • Event website, registration and app

    The event's own website and mobile app with agenda, speakers, maps and networking, plus registration, ticket types, payments and confirmations connected to your attendee list in real time.

  • Email campaigns and communications

    Invitations, reminders and follow-ups planned on a calendar and sent to the right segments.

  • Hotel room blocks and travel

    Negotiated room blocks, booking links and travel coordination for speakers and delegates.

  • Speaker and agenda management

    Invitations, bios, materials and session scheduling in one place, with confirmations tracked.

  • Sponsors and exhibitors

    Packages, contracts, booth assignments and deliverables managed from first contact to the show floor.

  • Master schedule and risk plan

    One schedule for every team and a plan B for the points most likely to fail.

What the team delivers

On-site operation

  • Check-in and accreditation

    Fast check-in with QR codes and badge printing, plus access control by attendee type.

  • Stage, rooms and technical crew

    Audio, video, lighting and interpretation coordinated room by room, with rehearsals before doors open.

  • Speaker and talent logistics

    Arrivals, transfers, green room and timing for every speaker, with a person assigned to each.

  • Live support team and incident handling

    A team on the floor and a central desk to solve incidents before attendees notice them.

  • Live app operation and notifications

    Real-time updates, session changes and attendee messages managed from the event desk by the same team.

Ideally three to six months ahead for a large conference, although we have delivered projects on shorter timelines.

Yes. We have delivered events in more than 15 countries.

How is a project priced?

Each event is quoted by scope: format, size and the services included. Your proposal itemizes every component.

Can you work with our venue and vendors?

Yes. We can coordinate your existing venue and suppliers, or recommend trusted ones.

What languages do you operate in?

We run events in English and Spanish.
