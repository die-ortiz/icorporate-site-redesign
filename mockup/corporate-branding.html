Frequently asked questions

Can you work with our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. We can extend and apply your current guidelines, or evolve them if they need an update.

Can you produce physical materials too?

Yes. We design and coordinate production of printed pieces, signage and merchandising.

Do you handle more than one language?

Yes. We design systems and materials that work in English and Spanish.

How is a branding project priced?

Each project is quoted by scope: the pieces involved, formats and number of reviews. The proposal itemizes everything.

Can you also handle signage for our events?

Yes. Because events are part of what we do, we can carry your identity through the whole event experience.
